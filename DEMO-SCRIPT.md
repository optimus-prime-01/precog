## RECORDING TIPS

1. **Screen resolution:** 1920x1080, browser fullscreen
2. **Browser:** Chrome dark mode, no bookmarks bar
3. **Speed:** Don't rush. Let the graph animate. Let predictions load.
4. **Pre-load:** Have a topic already loaded with 50+ entities before recording
5. **Voiceover:** Record voice separately for clean audio, or use AI voiceover
6. **Music:** Subtle background music (royalty-free, low volume)
7. **Length:** Keep under 5 minutes — judges have many submissions to review
8. **Show terminal briefly** — judges love seeing the backend work
